Appetiser
Orange Vinaigrette with
Sliced Chicken Salad
Equipment used
Zumex Versatile Star — Fresh juice extraction
Learn more
Hallde RG-100 — Vegetable prep & slicing
Learn more
Ingredients
2oranges, juiced
60mlextra virgin olive oil
1 tbspDijon mustard
1 tbsphoney
1 clovegarlic, minced
—salt & white pepper
300gchicken breast, cajun-rubbed & baked, chilled
2 cupsmixed salad greens
½cucumber, diced
—cherry tomatoes, halved
—mandarin segments, drained
—toasted almond flakes
Method
1
Juice the oranges using the Zumex Versatile Star. Combine juice with mustard, honey, garlic, salt and white pepper in a bottle. Seal and shake to emulsify. Add olive oil and shake again until combined.
2
Dice the cucumber uniformly using the Hallde RG-100 with the dicing blade attachment.
3
Thinly slice the chilled cajun chicken breast using the Hallde RG-100 slicer blade for even, consistent cuts.
4
Arrange greens, cucumber, cherry tomatoes and mandarin segments. Top with chicken slices, drizzle vinaigrette generously, and finish with toasted almonds. Optional: grate a little orange zest directly over the salad.
Chef's Tip
The Hallde RG-100 gives you perfectly uniform cuts every time — essential for even dressing absorption and consistent plating across every serving.
Main Course
Cashew Tofu Purée with
Crispy Shiitake & Chilli Crisp Oil
Equipment used
Vitamix Vita-Prep 3 — High-performance commercial blending
Learn more
Ingredients
200graw cashews, soaked overnight
150gsilken tofu (1 block)
—salt to taste
200gshiitake mushrooms, sliced
1 tbspneutral oil
—chilli crisp oil, to finish
—salt & black pepper
—chives (optional)
Method
1
Blend soaked cashews and silken tofu in the Vitamix Vita-Prep 3 on high for 90 seconds until completely smooth and thick. Season generously with salt. Add a touch more tofu for a looser purée if needed.
2
Heat oil in a pan until very hot. Sear sliced shiitake mushrooms without moving for 3–4 minutes until golden and crispy. Season with salt and black pepper.
3
Spread purée generously in a shallow bowl. Top with crispy shiitake, spoon chilli crisp oil over, and finish with cracked black pepper and chives if using.
Chef's Tip
The Vita-Prep 3's high-torque motor achieves a silkiness no standard blender can match — the friction heat also warms the purée perfectly for immediate plating.
Dessert
Waffles with Lemon Cheesecake
Drizzle & Fresh Fruits
Equipment used
Vitamix Vita-Prep 3 — High-performance commercial blending
Learn more
Ingredients
250gcream cheese, room temperature
110gcaster sugar
1lemon, juiced (zest reserved)
½ tspvanilla extract
4ready-made waffles, warmed
—strawberries, sliced
—blueberries
—mint, to garnish
Method
1
Blend cream cheese, sugar, lemon juice and vanilla in the Vitamix Vita-Prep 3 on medium speed until completely smooth and pourable. Do not over-blend. Finish with lemon zest stirred in by hand.
2
Warm waffles until just crisp on the outside. Drizzle or spoon the lemon cheesecake cream generously over each waffle.
3
Top with fresh strawberries and blueberries. Finish with a fine grating of lemon zest and a sprig of mint. Serve immediately.
Chef's Tip
Room temperature cream cheese is essential for a lump-free blend. The Vita-Prep 3's variable speed gives you precise control — stop as soon as it's smooth to keep the drizzle pourable and glossy.
Beverage
Mango Coconut Smoothie
Equipment used
Vitamix The Quiet One — Front-of-house bar blender
Learn more
Ingredients
300gfrozen mango chunks
150mlcoconut water
2 tbsphoney, or to taste
—pinch of sea salt
—mint, to garnish (optional)
—toasted coconut flakes (optional)
Method
1
Add coconut water, honey and a pinch of sea salt to the Vitamix The Quiet One first — liquid at the bottom ensures the blades catch cleanly.
2
Add frozen mango on top. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy. Taste and adjust sweetness with more honey.
3
Pour into chilled glasses. Garnish with mint and toasted coconut flakes if desired. Serve immediately.
Chef's Tip
The Quiet One is built for front-of-house use — powerful enough for frozen fruit, quiet enough for a demo floor. Frozen mango replaces ice so the smoothie stays thick and never watery.
