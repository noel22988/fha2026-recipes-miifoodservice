Appetiser
Orange Vinaigrette with
Sliced Chicken Salad
Hallde Vegetable Preparation Machine
Ingredients
2oranges, juiced
60mlextra virgin olive oil
1 tbspDijon mustard
1 tbsphoney
1 clovegarlic, minced
—salt & white pepper
300gchicken breast, cajun-rubbed & baked, chilled
2 cupsmixed salad greens
½cucumber, diced
—cherry tomatoes, halved
—canned mandarins, drained
—toasted almond flakes
Method
1
Combine orange juice, mustard, honey, garlic, salt and white pepper in a bottle. Seal and shake well to emulsify. Add olive oil and shake again until combined.
2
Use the Hallde machine to dice the cucumber uniformly.
3
Thinly slice the chilled cajun chicken breast using the Hallde slicer blade.
4
Arrange greens, cucumber, cherry tomatoes and mandarin segments. Top with chicken slices, drizzle vinaigrette generously, and finish with toasted almonds. Optional: zest a little orange directly over the salad.
Chef's Tip
The Hallde ensures every slice is identical — crucial for both plating consistency and even dressing absorption.

Main Course
Cashew Tofu Purée with
Crispy Shiitake & Chilli Crisp Oil
Vitamix Commercial Blender
Ingredients
200graw cashews, soaked
150gsilken tofu (1 block)
—salt to taste
200gshiitake mushrooms, sliced
1 tbspneutral oil
—chilli crisp oil
—salt & black pepper
—chives (optional)
Method
1
Blend soaked cashews and silken tofu in the Vitamix on high for 90 seconds until completely smooth and thick. Season generously with salt. Adjust consistency to taste — add a touch more tofu for a looser purée.
2
Slice shiitake mushrooms. Heat oil in a pan until hot, sear sliced mushrooms without moving for 3–4 min until golden and crispy. Season with salt and black pepper.
3
Spread purée generously in a shallow bowl. Top with crispy shiitake, spoon chilli crisp oil over, and finish with a crack of black pepper and chives if using.
Chef's Tip
The Vitamix achieves a silkiness no standard blender can match — the friction heating also warms the purée perfectly for serving.

Dessert
Waffles with Lemon Cheesecake
Drizzle & Fresh Fruits
Vitamix Commercial Blender
Ingredients
250gcream cheese, room temp
110gcaster sugar
1lemon, juiced (zest reserved)
½ tspvanilla extract
4ready-made waffles, warmed
—strawberries, sliced
—blueberries
—mint, to garnish
Method
1
Blend cream cheese, sugar, lemon juice and vanilla in the Vitamix on medium until completely smooth and pourable. Do not over-blend.
2
Warm waffles. Drizzle or spoon the lemon cheesecake cream generously over each waffle.
3
Top with fresh strawberries, blueberries and a sprig of mint. Finish with a fine grating of lemon zest over the top. Serve immediately.
Chef's Tip
Room temperature cream cheese is essential for a lump-free blend. The higher sugar ratio keeps the drizzle pourable and glossy.

Beverage
Mango Coconut Smoothie
Vitamix Commercial Blender
Ingredients
300gfrozen mango chunks
150mlcoconut water
2 tbsphoney, or to taste
—pinch of sea salt
—mint, to garnish (optional)
—toasted coconut flakes (optional)
Method
1
Add coconut water, honey and a pinch of sea salt to the Vitamix first.
2
Add frozen mango on top. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy. Taste and adjust sweetness with more honey.
3
Pour into chilled glasses. Garnish with mint and/or coconut flakes if desired. Serve immediately.
Chef's Tip
Frozen mango replaces ice — never add ice cubes or the smoothie goes watery. Keep it simple: the mango and coconut water do all the work. Adjust honey to the ripeness of your mango batch.
